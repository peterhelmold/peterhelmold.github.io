Research

Master’s thesisMarch — August 2026

Accelerating Convergence to Equilibrium in Gaussian Diffusions

Characterised linear diffusions preserving a prescribed Gaussian invariant measure via the Lyapunov equation, and analysed convergence in L²-Wasserstein distance through spectral properties of the drift matrix.

Compared optimal reversible and non-reversible convergence rates under noise constraints, including explicit low-dimensional constructions and the arbitrary-dimensional optimisation problem. Applied the framework to kinetic Langevin dynamics and examined extensions to strongly convex non-Gaussian potentials.

Supervisor: Dr. S. G. Cox
