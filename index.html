MSc Thesis · University of Amsterdam · 2026

Accelerating Convergence to Equilibrium in Gaussian Diffusions

Optimal Rates and Applications to Langevin Dynamics

Studied how noise allocation and non-reversible drift affect convergence of Ornstein–Uhlenbeck diffusions to a prescribed Gaussian equilibrium, reducing Wasserstein convergence to a spectral optimisation problem.

  1. Gave complete proofs of the two-dimensional optimal-rate results, made the optimal reversible comparison explicit, and determined equality cases for the fixed-drift problem.
  2. Reformulated the arbitrary-dimensional optimum under \(\operatorname{tr}(\mathbf{D}) \leq \eta\) as \(\frac{\eta}{d}\lambda_{\max}(\mathbf{C}^{-1})\) for a general noise budget and constructed an explicit three-dimensional optimiser.
  3. Compared overdamped, kinetic, and three-level Langevin dynamics and extended synchronous-coupling estimates to uniformly strongly convex non-Gaussian potentials.

Supervised by Dr. S. G. Cox

BSc Thesis · University of Liverpool · 2023

Random Walk and the Boundary Value Problems

Studied simple random walks on \(\mathbb{Z}^d\), including their probability distributions and the recurrence–transience dichotomy across dimensions.

Used stopping times and exit distributions to represent discrete Dirichlet solutions and prove uniqueness on finite domains and for bounded solutions under an exit-time condition.

Supervised by Dr. Y. Liu
